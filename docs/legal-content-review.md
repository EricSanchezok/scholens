# Public legal content review

The existing Privacy Policy and Terms of Service remain available only through
the legacy comparison client while authoritative replacement text is reviewed.
They must not be copied into the canonical Web application or treated as a
current engineering specification without approval from the service operator's
authorized business or legal owner.

Before publishing replacement text, confirm and record:

- the legal identity and contact details of the service operator;
- the production domains and countries in which the service is offered;
- account, research-content, telemetry, billing, support, and security data
  actually collected by the deployed product;
- each processor and provider that can receive personal data or user content;
- retention, export, account deletion, backup, and incident-response behavior;
- cookie, analytics, email, and consent behavior in each supported region;
- subscription, cancellation, refund, warranty, and service-availability terms;
- intellectual-property terms for uploaded papers, annotations, prompts, and
  generated outputs;
- cross-border transfer mechanisms and applicable privacy rights;
- governing law, dispute process, age restrictions, and effective date.

Approval must identify the reviewer, approved text revision, effective date,
and the canonical public URL. Engineering may then implement that approved text
without inventing or broadening legal commitments.
